# Onboarding

To start the pagoPA onboarding procedure, a PSP must sign an agreement with PagoPA S.p.A., which requires the PSP to pay a fee based on the number of transactions carried out.

To join the pagoPA system, the PSP's representative must download the agreement template form from the Payment Service Providers section of the PagoPA S.p.A. website, digitally sign it, and send it via certified email (PEC) to: [accordipsp@pec.pagopa.it](mailto:accordipsp@pec.pagopa.it)

For any clarification on the PSP onboarding procedure, you can consult the FAQs published on the PagoPA S.p.A. website or write to [legal@pagopa.it](mailto:legal@pagopa.it).

For the duration of the agreement, the affiliated PSP must be responsible for providing services related to the execution of payments on behalf of ECs and for technological intermediation (Activation of a PSP on the pagoPA system), when operating as a Technology Intermediary.

By signing the agreement, the affiliated PSP declares to have read and to fully and unconditionally accept the provisions of the Guidelines and their annexes, as well as other measures already issued by the Agency for Digital Italy for the use of the pagoPA platform, committing to full compliance with the provisions contained therein, as well as the provisions in any other related documentation issued pursuant to Article 5 of the CAD, and the provisions of the Regulations on the use of the registered pagoPA trademark and its annexes.

The affiliated PSP, in its capacity as a Technology Intermediary or for services for which it has not exercised the option to use a Technology Intermediary, undertakes to establish and maintain for the duration of the agreement an operational team connected with the helpdesk service that PagoPA S.p.A. provides to private users and ECs, in order to collaborate proactively with PagoPA S.p.A. and provide the latter with all information and support, as better specified in the Guidelines.

The affiliated PSP undertakes to ensure that its infrastructure is always technologically and applicatively adequate for providing the services under the agreement, to promptly carry out all interventions that may prove necessary or are requested by PagoPA S.p.A., and to guarantee strict compliance with the Guidelines in force.
