# Go-Live Process

## Activation of a PSP connecting directly to the Node <a href="#invedfdp2run" id="invedfdp2run"></a>

The representative of the PSP intending to activate on the pagoPA system through a direct connection to the platform must identify the most suitable solution to create a physical connection capable of guaranteeing the service levels required by PagoPA S.p.A. and must also provide for a disaster recovery plan. The go-live process for a PSP connecting directly to the payment platform involves meeting certain requirements:

- the activation of a physical connection to the platform's External Test environment;
- the activation of a physical connection to the platform's Production environment.

Once the connections are established, the PSP's representative can start the process by operating as follows:

1. fills out the Informative Data Catalog to be used in the External Test environment and, along with it, provides PagoPA S.p.A. with all the necessary information to complete the configuration of the Payment Channels indicated in the Catalog;
2. proceeds with the execution of the tests planned in the External Test environment, with or without the support of PagoPA S.p.A. staff for the formal validation of the results of the use cases indicated in the Test Plan (made available by PagoPA S.p.A. on the institutional website);
3. once the testing phase in the External Test environment is completed, the PSP's representative fills out the Informative Data Catalog and, along with it, provides PagoPA S.p.A. with all the necessary information to complete the configuration of the Payment Channels in the Production environment;
4. proceeds with the execution of the tests planned in that environment, with or without the support of PagoPA S.p.A. staff for the formal validation of the results of the use cases indicated in the Test Plan;
5. provides PagoPA S.p.A. with information regarding the "operational desk".

To complete the activation process, the PSP's representative fills out the waiver document for the execution of the services covered by the test cases and delivers it to PagoPA S.p.A. duly signed. By delivering the waiver, the representative guarantees to have successfully completed, in both the External Test and Production environments, all test cases required by PagoPA S.p.A. as of the date the document is signed.

## Activation of a PSP acting as an Acquirer <a href="#keca5uub56eg" id="keca5uub56eg"></a>

The activation of a PSP intending to act as an Acquirer involves the PSP indicating whether it will use a proprietary Payment Gateway or the VPOS service from SIA SpA.

## Activation of a PSP offering the CBILL service <a href="#y4fovt50y64o" id="y4fovt50y64o"></a>

PSPs intending to offer end-users the CBILL service (from the CBI - Customer to Business Interaction consortium) on the pagoPA system have a simplified activation process, as the CBI Consortium acts as the Technological Intermediary, and the PSP's only responsibility is to send PagoPA S.p.A. the "Informative Data Catalog" along with all information related to the CBILL payment channel.

## Activation of an intermediated PSP <a href="#smow7ueaopam" id="smow7ueaopam"></a>

Participating PSPs that intend to use the pagoPA system indirectly can use intermediaries to whom they delegate all technical activities. For these activities, the "intermediated" PSP will refer to the technical figure designated by the chosen technological intermediary, without the power to appoint or replace them due to the delegation of technical activities.
