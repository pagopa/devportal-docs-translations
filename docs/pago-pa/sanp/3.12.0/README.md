# Foreword

**Annex B to the "Guidelines for making electronic payments to public administrations and public service providers" published by PagoPA S.p.A. - version 3.12.0-May 2026**

The electronic payment system for the Public Administration, the pagoPA system, guarantees end-users the ability to make electronic payments to the Public Administration, state-controlled companies, and Public Service Providers in a secure, reliable, and simple way, and with full transparency in commission costs.

The introduction of the pagoPA platform brings benefits for citizens, the Public Administration, Payment Service Providers, and the entire country system:

- benefits for Citizens
  - transparency and lower costs;
  - ability to use public services more immediately;
  - simplification of the payment process, allowing the use of the largest possible number of channels and services;
  - standardization of the user experience for payments to the Public Administration, state-controlled companies, and Public Service Providers;
  - standardization of payment notice communications;
- benefits for the Public Administration
  - reduction in collection times by crediting the amounts directly to their accounts by the day after payment;
  - reduction of cash handling costs;
  - improved efficiency in collections management through automatic reconciliation;
  - overcoming the need to issue tenders to acquire collection services, with consequent reductions in inefficiencies and uncompetitive commission costs;
  - reduction in the cost and time of developing online applications (solution reuse);
  - elimination of the need for multiple collection agreements;
  - greater automatic controls to prevent double payments and the resulting refund procedures;
- benefits for Payment Service Providers (PSPs)
  - elimination of the need for multiple agreements with PAs;
  - reduction of cash handling costs;
  - improvement of services rendered;
  - customer loyalty;
- benefits for the Country System
  - full compliance with PSD2 standards;
  - incentivizing the use of electronic payments at a national level for transactions with the Public Administration, which helps stimulate the market and foster greater competition among payment services and, consequently, a leveling of commissions.

The pagoPA platform was created by the Agency for Digital Italy (AgID) in implementation of art. 5 of the CAD, which specifies that in order to implement the provisions of the same article, "the Presidency of the Council of Ministers provides, through the Public Connectivity System, a technological platform for the interconnection and interoperability between public administrations and authorized payment service providers". The same article 5 of the CAD also entrusted the Agency for Digital Italy, after consulting the Bank of Italy, with the task of defining the Guidelines for the implementation of the same article 5 of the CAD and for the specification of payment identification codes and the methods by which the payment service provider makes information relating to the payment available to the entity.

The [D.L. 135/2018](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2018/12/14/18G00163/sg) transferred the management of the pagoPA platform, as well as the tasks related to this platform carried out by the Agency for Digital Italy, to the Presidency of the Council of Ministers and ordered the establishment of a state-owned joint-stock company that operates under the direction of the President of the Council of Ministers, namely PagoPA S.p.A.

This document, named “Implementation Specifications of the SPC Payments Hub,” represents Annex B to the “Guidelines for making payments to public administrations and public service providers” (hereinafter, Guidelines) and must be used in conjunction with the document “Implementation specifications for payment, transfer, and reporting identification codes” (Annex A), as well as with the Guidelines themselves; documents to which reference is made for all items and topics not specifically indicated herein.
