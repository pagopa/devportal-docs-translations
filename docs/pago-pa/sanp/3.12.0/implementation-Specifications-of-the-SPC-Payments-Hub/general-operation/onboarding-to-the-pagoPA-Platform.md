# Joining the pagoPA platform

The pagoPA payment system is provided for in Article 5 of the CAD referred to in Legislative Decree. Lgs 82/2005 and by law (pursuant to the combined provisions of art. 2, paragraph 2 of the CAD and art. 15, paragraph 5-bis, of Law Decree). 179/2012) all Public Administrations must use it exclusively, decommissioning other collection payment systems.

The Legislative Decree. no. 179/2016 (Official Gazette. no. 214 of 13.9.2016) and Legislative Decree no. 217/2017 (Official Gazette. no. 9 of 12.01.2018) have respectively amended and corrected Article 2, paragraph 2, of the CAD, introducing into the subjective scope of the CAD also public service operators and state-controlled companies, as defined in the legislative decree adopted in implementation of Article 18 of Law no. 124 of 2015, excluding listed companies.

The Legislative Decree. no. 175/2016, in Article 2, letter m), outlined the concept of a state-controlled company. In particular, state-controlled companies are defined as those companies in which one or more public administrations exercise control powers pursuant to Article 2359 of the Civil Code, namely:

1. companies in which another company holds the majority of the votes that can be cast in the ordinary shareholders' meeting;
2. companies in which another company has sufficient votes to exercise a dominant influence in the ordinary shareholders' meeting;
3. companies that are under the dominant influence of another company by virtue of particular contractual ties with it.

Article 2359 of the Civil Code specifies that for the purposes of applying numbers (1) and (2) above, votes belonging to subsidiary companies, trust companies, and intermediaries are also counted; votes held on behalf of third parties are not counted. Companies over which another company exercises significant influence are considered associated companies. Influence is presumed when at least one-fifth of the votes, or one-tenth if the company has shares listed on the stock exchange, can be exercised in the ordinary shareholders' meeting”. Finally, in Article 2 of the Legislative Decree. no. 175/2016 it is further specified that “Control may also exist when, in application of legal or statutory provisions or shareholder agreements, unanimous consent of all parties sharing control is required for strategic financial and management decisions relating to the company's business”.

Finally, it should be noted that entities obliged to use pagoPA, whether they are PAs, public service operators, or state-controlled companies, if they meet all the requirements (none excluded) indicated in the exemption letter, can apply to PagoPA S.p.A. (by email to [pagamenti@assistenza.pagopa.it](mailto:pagamenti@assistenza.pagopa.it)), by sending the duly completed and digitally signed exemption letter as an attachment.

Payment Service Providers (PSPs), such as banks, post offices, electronic money institutions, payment institutions, and any other entity authorized to perform payment services, join the pagoPA system on a voluntary basis to provide their payment services to citizens and businesses.

Joining the Nodo dei Pagamenti-SPC is reserved for ECs and PSPs who intend to use the Nodo dei Pagamenti-SPC directly or through an intermediary.

In the event that the joining entity uses intermediaries to carry out the technical interfacing activities with the pagoPA platform, it would still remain responsible as the logical sender or recipient of the information flows.
