# Payment at the CE's frontend

This process is activated when the payment operation is initiated from the frontend of a CE. The workflow aims to have the least possible impact on CEs and PSPs; in fact, the communication interfaces are the same as those used for payment at PSPs, so they share all the same assumptions.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-03-20 alle 11.52.57.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

- when the CE's frontend receives a payment request for one or more notices, it forwards it with a [redirect](../creditor-entity/integration-methods/ec-touchpoint-integration-with-Checkout.md) to Checkout, the frontend interface of PagoPA S.p.A.;
- Checkout asks the Nodo to activate the _n_ payments it has received (a maximum of 5) at the CE;
- each individual payment activation request reaches the CE via the [paGetPayment](../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pagetpayment);
- Checkout allows the participating PSP, which offers its digital payment instruments within the pagoPA platform ([offrire-sistemi-di-pagamento-su-touchpoints-di-pagopa-s.p.a..md](../prestatore-di-servizi-di-pagamento/modalita-di-integrazione/offrire-sistemi-di-pagamento-su-touchpoints-di-pagopa-s.p.a..md "mention")), to collect the sum owed by the user;
- once the payment operations are concluded, Checkout performs a redirect to the CE's frontend and sends the outcomes to the Nodo, which then sends them to the PSP via the [pspNotifyPayment vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2), in case of a KO response from the PSP, the process is interrupted and the payment must be reversed;
- in case of technical exceptions during the call to the [pspNotifyPayment vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2) that prevent a response from being received, the Nodo would wait for the first of the following events to occur
  - _payment token expiration_: the process is interrupted and the payment must be reversed;
  - _receipt of the_ [_sendPaymentOutcome vers. 2_](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome-versione-2)_:_ the flow proceeds normally, always keeping in mind that the PSP cannot send an outcome = KO;
- if the PSP were to send a [sendPaymentOutcome vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome-versione-2) after responding with a KO to the [pspNotifyPayment vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2) the Nodo would respond with a KO to signal the discordant outcome;
- in case of acceptance of the [pspNotifyPayment vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2) the PSP is required to provide the payment outcome **within 2 sec** of acceptance with the [sendPaymentOutcome vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome-versione-2), which contains a single outcome for all tokens activated in the previous phases;
- if the PSP were to send an outcome = KO after accepting the [pspNotifyPayment vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2) the Nodo would respond with a KO to signal the discordant outcome;
- via the [paSendRT](../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pasendrt) primitive, the _receipt_ (receipt) is forwarded to the _n_ ECs involved in the payment only if the payment has been made; the _receipt_ is an object generated by the pagoPA platform;
- when the CE receives the _receipt_, it must close the debt position and consider the related payment notice as fully settled.

For error handling, refer to [Error Management](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/mU2qgiLV1G3m9z1VjAOc/ "mention").

For a correct and standardized use of _metadata_, a specific [Metadata Dictionary](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/u6YdY319vyFX9MIvnKBa/ "mention") has been created, in which there is a section dedicated to payment channel information present in the _additionalPaymentInformations_ of the [pspNotifyPayment vers. 2](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2).
