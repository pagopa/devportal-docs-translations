# Payment of a notice at a PSP

This process involves the payment of a notice previously issued by an EC at the infrastructures provided by the PSP, such as Banks and ATMs, Post Offices and Postal Points, Bars, Newsstands, Betting Shops, Supermarkets, Tobacconists, and other affiliated Merchants.

The current payment workflow at PSPs aims to manage requests from PSPs, where possible internally to the platform, by overcoming:

- the availability and latency of ECs, providing additional services where necessary;
- the availability of essential information to proceed with the payment collection;
- the possibility of concurrent payments.

- The [verifyPaymentNotice](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#activatepaymentnotice) is an **OPTIONAL** service that does not change the status of the debt position, usable by PSPs that initiate the payment via the QR code on the paper notice or by manually entering the data;
- the [verificaBollettino](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#verificabollettino) is an **OPTIONAL** service that does not change the status of the debt position, usable exclusively by the PSP Poste Italiane, which initiates the payment via the Data Matrix on the paper notice, and not via the QR Code;
- the [paVerifyPaymentNotice](../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#paverifypaymentnotice) is used to request the EC to verify the payment option identified by the notice number;
- the [verifyPaymentOptions](../appendici/primitive/psp/api-rest/#pagetpayment) is an **OPTIONAL** service that does not change the status of the debt position, usable by PSPs that initiate the payment via the QR code on the paper notice or by manually entering data, and it provides Creditor Entities (ECs) with a feature to display payment options, such as installment plans, surcharges, or reductions in the amount;
- The [paVerifyPaymentOptions](../appendici/primitive/ente-creditore/api-rest/#pagetpayment) service is used to request the EC to provide the available payment options. To this end, a flag has been added to the station configuration to determine if the service can return the payment options. If the EC manages these options ("new Verify" flag set to TRUE), the options will be returned to the citizen via the PSP's touchpoint from which the request originated.
- with the [activatePaymentNotice](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#activatepaymentnotice-1), the PSP asks the node to activate the payment at the EC; this phase is mandatory for PSPs;
- the payment activation request reaches the EC via the [paGetPayment](../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pagetpayment);
- the PSP is MANDATORILY required to provide the payment outcome in real time with the [sendPaymentOutcome](../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome), i.e., as soon as the end-user has paid or confirmed on the PSP's touchpoint and in any case no later than the token's expiration, both in the case of a successful payment (outcome = OK) and a failed payment (outcome = KO);
- via the [paSendRT](../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pasendrt) primitive, the _receipt_ (payment receipt) is forwarded to the _n_ ECs involved in the payment only if the payment has been made; the _receipt_ is an object generated by the pagoPA platform;
- when the EC receives the _receipt_, it must close the debt position and consider it fully paid.

For error handling, refer to [Error Handling](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/mU2qgiLV1G3m9z1VjAOc/ "mention").
