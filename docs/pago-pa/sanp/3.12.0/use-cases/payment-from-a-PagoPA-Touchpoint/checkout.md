# Checkout

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/checkout_SANP (3).png>)

- the user initiating the payment from Checkout must enter the payment notice number and the identifier of the recipient EC;
- the [paVerifyPaymentNotice](../../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#paverifypaymentnotice) is used to request the EC to verify the payment option identified by the payment notice number;
- the payment activation request reaches the EC through the [paGetPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pagetpayment);
- once the user has completed the actual payment operations, the Hub sends the details to the PSP via the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment); in case of a KO response from the PSP, the process is interrupted and the payment must be reversed;
- in case of technical exceptions during the call to [pspNotifyPayment ver. 2](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2) that prevent a response from being received, the Hub will wait for the first of the following events to occur:
  - payment token expiration: the process is interrupted and the payment must be reversed;
  - receipt of the [sendPaymentOutcome ver. 2](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome-versione-2): the flow proceeds normally, always keeping in mind that the PSP cannot send an outcome = KO;
- if the PSP sends a [sendPaymentOutcome](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome-versione-2) after responding with a KO to the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment), the Hub would respond with a KO to signal the conflicting outcome;
- in case of acceptance of the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment), the PSP is required to provide the payment outcome **within 2 sec** with the [sendPaymentOutcome](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome-versione-2); if the PSP sends an outcome = KO, the Hub would respond with a KO to signal the conflicting outcome;
- via the [paSendRT](../../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pasendrt) primitive, the _receipt_ (receipt) is forwarded to the _n_ ECs involved in the payment only if the payment has been completed. The _receipt_ is an object generated by the pagoPA platform;
- when the EC receives the _receipt_, it must close the debt position and consider it fully paid.

For error handling, refer to [Error Handling](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/mU2qgiLV1G3m9z1VjAOc/ "mention").

For a correct and standardized use of _metadata_, a dedicated [Metadata Dictionary](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/u6YdY319vyFX9MIvnKBa/ "mention") has been created, which contains a section dedicated to the payment channel information present in the _additionalPaymentInformations_ of the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment-versione-2).
