# Technological Intermediary

## Who can act as a Technological Intermediary?

As provided for by the operating model, **both Creditor Entities and Payment Service Providers, to join pagoPA, can benefit from the interface activities already put in place by other participating entities (so-called Technological Intermediaries**). The technological intermediary is an entity that, after joining the pagoPA platform, offers others a service for connecting and exchanging data flows with the pagoPA platform, in full compliance with the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2018/07/03/18A04494/sg) and their technical standards; therefore, only entities that are already members of the Nodo dei Pagamenti-SPC can act as technological intermediaries.

## How can a PSP use an Intermediary?

As provided for in art. 6, paragraph 2, of the membership agreement, **the individual PSP can designate a Technological Intermediary for each payment service it provides.** In the information data catalogue, the PSP indicates all the necessary information. Therefore, to choose an Intermediary, the PSP must provide this indication to PagoPA S.p.A. by sending a certified email (PEC) to [accordipsp@pec.pagopa.it](mailto:accordipsp%40pec.pagopa.it).
