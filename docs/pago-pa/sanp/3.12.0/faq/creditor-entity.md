# Creditor Entity

## What are the deadlines for the exclusive use of the pagoPA Platform?

Article 65, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree no. 217 of 13 December 2017, as amended by Article 1, paragraph 8, of Law Decree 162/2019, states that "**The obligation for authorised payment service providers to exclusively use the platform** referred to in Article 5, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree no. 82 of 2005 **for payments to public administrations shall apply from 28 February 2021**. Also, in order to enable digital payments by citizens, **the subjects referred to in Article 2, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree No. 82 of 7 March 2005, are required, by 28 February 2021, to integrate their collection systems with the platform** referred to in Article 5, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree No. 82 of 7 March 2005, or to avail themselves, for this purpose, of services provided by other subjects referred to in the same Article 2, paragraph 2, or by collection service providers already enabled to operate on the platform.

Failure to comply with the obligation in the preceding paragraph is relevant for the measurement and evaluation of the individual performance of the responsible managers and entails managerial and disciplinary liability pursuant to Articles 21 and 55 of Legislative Decree No. 165 of 30 March 2001". **Therefore, after 28 February 2021, PSPs authorised to operate in Italy by the Bank of Italy will not be able to execute any payment services that do not pass through the pagoPA System, where the beneficiary is one of the subjects under Article 2, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree no. 82 of 7 March 2005.**

Public administrations and the other subjects mentioned in Article 2, paragraph 2, by 28 February 2021, as they can no longer collect payments through a PSP outside the pagoPA System, must have opted for one or more of the following solutions for the full management of their collections via pagoPA: integration of their collection systems with the pagoPA Platform; use of collection services provided by other beneficiary subjects already active on the pagoPA Platform; or entrusting their revenues to a special collection agent that is already a member of pagoPA.

## Are administrations obliged to publish the IBANs of their bank accounts?

No. Given the national importance of pagoPA as the single platform for managing collections, entities required to join pagoPA cannot ask users for payments via bank transfers that are not integrated with the pagoPA System. For this very reason, paragraph 5 of the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) specifies that "to prevent users from making bank transfers not integrated with the pagoPA System, entities legally required to join pagoPA are forbidden from publishing the receiving IBAN in any way. It is understood that if a user, however, having stored this IBAN, makes a transfer outside of pagoPA, such a payment must still be managed by the creditor entity as a single exception, with the hope that such exceptions will become fewer over time.”.

Compliance with this provision is even more stringent as it affects both PAs and PSPs, given the prohibition for PSPs from 28 February 2021 to execute transactions outside the pagoPA System.

Therefore, Creditor Entities that are members of the pagoPA System must adapt their forms to remove any explicit reference to the IBAN for payment. It is understood that if a user, having stored this IBAN, makes a transfer outside the pagoPA System, this payment must still be managed by the Creditor Entity as a single exception if the PSP was unable to block it, with the hope that such exceptions will become fewer over time, to achieve full compliance with the law by PSPs and Creditor Entities.

## What are the legal references regarding electronic payments to the PA?

In light of Article 5 of the Digital Administration Code of Legislative Decree. no. 82/2005, the entities indicated in Article 2 of the same Code are obliged to accept, through the pagoPA platform, payments due for any reason through electronic payment systems. **To achieve the objectives of rationalisation and containment of public spending on information technology, and in order to guarantee uniformity of supply and high levels of security, Public Administrations** - pursuant to Article 15, paragraph 5 bis, of Law Decree no. 179/2012, as converted into law - are required to use the public **technological infrastructure**, better known as the **pagoPA** System, or also, the **pagoPA** platform, made available by PagoPA S.p.A..

The document "Guidelines for making electronic payments to Public Administrations and Public Service Providers", as updated and published in the Official Gazette from time to time, defines the rules and procedures for making electronic payments through the pagoPA System by its members. The Guidelines, as secondary legislation, are based on primary payment regulations, including the national legislation transposing PSD2.

## How should the concept of public administration be defined?

For the definition of Public Administration, please refer to the detailed information provided by the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Presidency of the Council of Ministers in interpretative circular no. 1 of 9 March 2015, issued for the subjective scope of [electronic invoicing](https://www.fatturapa.gov.it/it/norme-e-regole/normativa/).

## Is interconnection possible between the pagoPA System and international payment circuits?

In line with the pagoPA System's objective to guarantee a free market for PSPs, the Guidelines for making payments to public administrations and public service providers state that non-national PSPs can also join the System to provide payment services to users of the Italian PA, provided that the SEPA payment processes mentioned in the Guidelines themselves are respected. The system does not alter the processes defined for treasury management. Therefore, the PA is bound in its financial management, having to, if central, use the treasury service provided by the Bank of Italy and, if local, entrust its management to a treasurer and/or cashier and respect the principle of centralization referred to in articles 209 and 211 of the T.U.E.L.

## Are payment orders made via pagoPA revocable?

The PSD and PSD2, and their respective national implementation laws, generally establish the irrevocability of a payment order once it has been received by the PSP. Applying this regulatory provision to the payment methods of the pagoPA System, **once the payer has sent the request to the PSP to execute the payment transaction** (regardless of the instrument: bank transfer, credit card, cash, MyBank, etc.) **the payment cannot be revoked by the payer**.

## From when does the discharging effect for the payment of highway code fines made through pagoPA take effect?

As is known, Art. 202 of the Highway Code provides for a period of 5 and 60 days for the payment of the fine at a reduced rate. Regarding the discharging effect of payments for Highway Code fines, decree-law no. 18 of 14 February 2016, converted into law no. 49 of 8 April 2016, intervened, which in Art. 17 quinquies provides that: “the first and second periods of paragraph 1 of article 202 of the highway code, referred to in legislative decree no. 285 of 30 April 1992, are interpreted to mean that, for payments other than in cash or via postal current account, the discharging effect of the payment occurs if the credit in favor of the administration takes place within two days of the payment due date”.

That said, it is appropriate to take into due consideration that the aforementioned regulation, by referring to the two days necessary for the crediting of the requested payment transaction in the interbank system, refers to the legal deadline (D+1) established by PSD1 and confirmed by PSD2, and introduced into our legal system by Legislative Decree no. 11/2010 transposing the directive. Therefore, this regulation refers to the concept of an operating day, which differs from a working day, also in terms of the duration of the former compared to the latter.

Given the above, it should be pointed out that Art. 17 quinquies, as it concerns the terms of a payment transaction, with the expression 'two days' refers to two operating days and not working days, with the effect that neither Saturday, nor public holidays including Sunday, are operating days and that, therefore, for the calculation of these two days, neither Saturday, nor Sunday, nor the other public holidays of the year should be counted. Furthermore, it is also appropriate to point out that Art. 17 quinquies of decree-law no. 18 of 14 February 2016, converted into law no. 49 of 8 April 2016, makes no reference to the pagoPA System.

## How should the obligation to join pagoPA be applied for public service providers or for state-controlled companies?

Public service providers and/or state-controlled companies as defined in legislative decree no. 175 of 19 August 2016, excluding listed companies referred to in Article 2, paragraph 1, letter p), of the same decree that do not fall into the category referred to in letter b), must join the pagoPA platform, but this obligation does NOT imply the exclusive use of the pagoPA platform's payment services. In fact, unlike public administrations (cf. art. 5, paragraph 2-quater, of the CAD and art. 65, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree no. 217/2017), **public service providers and the listed companies referred to in art. 2 of the CAD must make payment services available to users via pagoPA but may also offer - in parallel to these services - other payment services not integrated with pagoPA**. It will, therefore, be up to the user making the payment to decide whether to use the payment services offered by pagoPA or the others offered directly by the beneficiary.

## What are the consequences of not joining pagoPA?

\*\*PAs that have not yet activated this system show inconsistencies in how they collect sums due. **With decree-law 76 of 2020, where specific penalties are provided for in case of delay, Art. 24: "La violazione dell’articolo 64, comma 3-bis e delle disposizioni di cui al presente articolo, costituisce mancato raggiungimento di uno specifico risultato e di un rilevante obiettivo da parte dei dirigenti responsabili delle strutture competenti e comporta la riduzione, non inferiore al 30 per cento della retribuzione di risultato e del trattamento accessorio collegato alla performance individuale dei dirigenti competenti, oltre al divieto di attribuire premi o incentivi nell’ambito delle medesime strutture"**. Furthermore, Art. 23-bis of the same decree-law provides that "_In order to introduce procedural simplification measures aimed at streamlining procedures and eliminating bureaucratic overlaps, while still taking into account technical-infrastructural difficulties and the repercussions on work organization following the containment measures for the global health emergency from COVID-19, municipalities with a population of less than 5,000 inhabitants shall comply with the provisions of this chapter for the support and dissemination of digital administration starting from the date set for the end of the state of emergency due to the health risk associated with the onset of diseases deriving from transmissible viral agents, established by a resolution of the Council of Ministers on 29 July 2020_)_".

## What precautions and instructions must be taken into account for generating the IUV code?

The IUV code is a structural element of the entire pagoPA System that allows the payment to be tracked throughout its entire life cycle. Please note that:

- for payments at the Creditor Entity, the IUV can be represented by an alphanumeric string of up to 35 characters. The use of the ISO 11649 standard is recommended;
- for payments at the PSP, the IUV is included within the Notice Code, and is represented by a string of up to 18 exclusively numeric characters. For more details, please refer to the “[Implementation specifications for payment, transfer, and reporting identification codes](https://developer.pagopa.it/pago-pa/guides/saci/3.2.1/specifiche-attuative-dei-codici-identificativi-di-versamento-riversamento-e-rendicontazione/generazione-dellidentificativo-univoco-di-versamento)”.

## Does joining pagoPA by an entity to which a PA has entrusted the collection of revenues affect the contracting PA itself?

**A Collection Agent** - defined as an entity registered in the register referred to in Article 53, paragraph 1, of Legislative Decree. no. 446/1997, which allows PAs to entrust the activities of assessment, settlement, and collection of revenues to third parties - **if it joins the pagoPA system, it may act as a Creditor Entity and beneficiary of electronic payment transactions carried out through the pagoPA platform only and exclusively if the PA has assigned the credit to it**. Therefore, not in the case where the Collection Agent is managing a credit owned by the taxing authority (as specified in Circular no. 3/DF of the Ministry of Economy and Finance). It is therefore specified that entrusting a Collection Agent is not among the reasons for exemption from joining the pagoPA platform by the contracting PA/taxing authority; the Collection Agent may freely decide to join the pagoPA system as a Partner/Technological Intermediary to facilitate the taxing authorities in joining the pagoPA platform; **the Collection Agent, for the collection of credits acquired from the PA following a credit assignment, may join pagoPA on its own behalf as a Public Service Provider-Creditor Entity**.

## Can a Creditor Entity use other electronic payment methods in addition to the payment services offered by pagoPA?

**The pagoPA platform is the national system for electronic payments to Public Administrations and other entities that provide public services and are legally required to join**. As provided in paragraph 5 of the Guidelines, Creditor Entities required to join pagoPA may use only the following payment methods alongside the system:

- «Delega unica F24» (so-called. F24 form) until its integration with the pagoPA System;
- SEPA Direct Debit (SDD) until its integration with the pagoPA System;
- any other payment services not yet integrated with the pagoPA System that cannot be replaced by those provided through pagoPA because a specific legal provision requires them to be made available to users for making payments;
- in cash, at the entity that performs the treasury or cash service for that entity.

Furthermore, to achieve the objectives of rationalization and containment of public spending, creditor entities are obliged to decommission any other electronic payment method not interconnected with the pagoPA System, except as specified in the preceding paragraph. It should also be noted that Article 65, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree no. 217 of 13 December 2017 establishes the obligation for authorized payment service providers to exclusively use the platform referred to in Article 5, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree no. 82 of 2005 for payments to public administrations starting from 28 February 2021.

Therefore, from that date, providers authorized to offer payment services under PSD2 will not be able to execute any payment services that do not pass through the pagoPA System and that have as a beneficiary a public entity obliged to join the same system, with the sole exception of the services referred to in letters a), b), c), and d) just mentioned.

Finally, it should be noted that, pursuant to Article 2, point 39, of Legislative Decree no. 218 of 15 December 2017 for the transposition of PSD2 in Italy, it is established that Articles 36, 37, and 38 of Legislative Decree no. 11 of 27 January 2010 are repealed from the date of entry into force of this decree, with the exception of paragraph 6 of Article 37, which is repealed starting from 1 January 2019. **Therefore, as Public Administrations must apply the provisions on payments established by PSD2 starting from 1 January 2019,** it is appropriate to state that, for the collections component alone, joining the pagoPA System also ensures full compliance with the European directive, as transposed at the national level.

## Can a Creditor Entity register IBANs on the pagoPA system for bank accounts not held in its name?

As detailed in the FAQ 'How to manage the relationship between a Creditor Entity and a Delegated Collection Agent?' each Creditor Entity on the pagoPA System is required - for managing collections related to the services provided - to register at least one IBAN of a bank account held in its name.

However, situations may arise where the Creditor Entity can also register IBANs of bank accounts not held in its name, upon sending a specific declaration via email (areariservata@assistenza.pagopa.it) and subject to a feasibility check by PagoPA S.p.A. itself. These IBANs must, however, belong to third parties that have an existing relationship with the Creditor Entity for the provision of specific services, and, at the same time, have a telematic connection that, even if outside the pagoPA System, allows the third party to receive the information flows exchanged on the pagoPA System.

The cases in which a Creditor Entity can register IBANs for bank accounts not held in its name are described below:

- A Creditor Entity wishing to register the IBANs of its controlled companies on the pagoPA System (e.g.). an in-house company of the Municipality);
- A Creditor Entity that, as a Union of Municipalities, wishes to register the IBANs of its member Entities on the pagoPA system;
- A Creditor Entity that, as a company with a majority shareholding from a Public Administration, wishes to register an IBAN held by the Public Administration itself, since the latter is the beneficiary of the credit.

These types are to be considered the only cases for which the registration of third-party IBANs is possible with respect to the current legislation on delegated collection.

## Who can act as a Technological Intermediary?

As provided for in the operating model, both Creditor Entities and Payment Service Providers, in order to join pagoPA, can benefit from the interfacing activities already carried out by other adhering entities (so-called. Technological Intermediaries). The Technological Intermediary is an entity belonging to the Public Administration that offers other adhering entities - after joining the pagoPA platform - a technological service for connecting and exchanging flows with the pagoPA platform, in full compliance with the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) and related technical standards. Therefore, only entities that have already joined the pagoPA System can act as Technological Intermediaries.

## If the Entity already has an active online payment system, can it use the pagoPA logo?

**Joining pagoPA is mandatory regardless of whether the Entity already has electronic payment methods available to its users.** The creation, in fact, of a centralized national system, responds to the broader objective referred to in Article 15, paragraph 5-bis, of Decree-Law. no. 179/2012, of rationalizing and containing public spending on IT, as well as that of ensuring homogeneity in the services offered to users and high levels of security.

That being said, **it should be noted that any online payment platform already created and/or in use by an Entity or a public service provider can be maintained as long as it is integrated with the pagoPA System for the exchange of related flows as described in the** [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG). The “pagoPA” logo, which identifies membership in the pagoPA System, is issued only to entities that have completed all the formalities required by the joining procedure (the documentation is available on the pagoPA website, divided by Creditor Entities and Payment Service Providers) and in accordance with the Guidelines for the Use of Trademarks available on the Company's institutional website, accessible at the following link: [LG\_007\_Linee Guida per l’uso dei Marchi di PagoPA S.p.A. rev. 1.1.docx](https://d2pqccwhek19jq.cloudfront.net/media/LG_007_Linee_Guida_per_l_uso_dei_Marchi_di_Pago_PA_S_p_A_rev_1_1_docx_e58652e1e9.pdf). Through this logo, in fact, users will be able to immediately understand if a public entity - whether as a beneficiary or as a payment service provider - has joined the pagoPA System.

## Are pension funds required to join pagoPA?

Yes. Recalling that the CAD was first amended by Legislative Decree. no. 179/2016 (Official Gazette. no. 214 of 13.9.2016) and subsequently corrected by D. Decree. no. 217/2017 (Official Gazette. no. 9 of 12.01.2018), **it should be noted that the current Article 2, paragraph 2, of the CAD, in addition to Public Administrations, has also introduced state-controlled companies and public service providers into the subjective scope of the CAD**.

Therefore, Public Administrations, state-controlled companies, and public service providers are required to join the pagoPA system to allow their users to make electronic payments to them in a uniform manner.

Without prejudice to the foregoing, it is necessary to recall that in our legal system, although identifying the legal nature of pension funds may create some interpretative doubt, in the specific case of joining the pagoPA system, it is appropriate to recall that this obligation also concerns the Entities included in the list referred to in Art. 1, paragraph 2, of Law. no. 196/2009 and even private entities managing public services. In this case, **since the national social security and welfare institutions are both included in the list referred to in Art. 1, paragraph 2, of Law. no. 196/2009, and are private entities managing public services, pursuant to Article 2, paragraph 2, letters c) and b) respectively, they are required to join the pagoPA system**.

## Are fund transfers between public entities made through treasury accounts at the Bank of Italy excluded from the pagoPA System?

Yes. The pagoPA System does not modify or alter the application of public finance regulations, including Art. 44 of Law no. 526/1982, which requires entities holding funds in current accounts or special accounts at the State Treasuries to execute fund transfers using these current or special accounts held by the recipient entities of the payments. All other forms of collection must be managed on the pagoPA platform.

## Are private healthcare facilities operating under accreditation with the national health service required to join pagoPA?

**When a private healthcare facility obtains accreditation, it operates as a public service provider**.

Consequently, it **is required to join pagoPA** as this obligation, pursuant to Art. 2, paragraph 2, of the CAD, also involves public service providers.

## Can a Local Authority choose to make only the payment methods offered by the pagoPA system available to users?

The pagoPA System is the national system for electronic payments to Public Administrations and other entities required to join the System. **Therefore, entities subject to joining the pagoPA infrastructure, in order to collect what is due to them, must make available to users the payment methods offered by the pagoPA platform, which can be supplemented by the cash payment service, at the Entity and/or the party that provides the treasury and cash service for that Entity**. Recalling what has just been stated, a Local Authority may make only the payment methods offered by the pagoPA system available to users, autonomously, in compliance with current legislation, according to its own management and accounting autonomy choices, in order to eliminate the use of cash and/or fully digitize the management of collections.

In consideration of the above, it should be recalled that the pagoPA platform provides the payer with the choice between making the payment online (including via mobile app) or at the counter.

## Voluntary associations (e.g.) Unions or Consortia of Municipalities) between local public bodies required to join the pagoPA platform?

**Yes. Neither the CAD, nor the D.L. no. 179/2012 provide for any type of exception and/or derogation in this regard**. In fact, any Entity that receives payments in its favor from private individuals or public bodies that cannot make the payment via a fund transfer operation at the Bank of Italy treasury, must implement the legal obligation to join the pagoPA platform.

## Are state-controlled companies that do not receive payments from citizens or businesses required to join pagoPA?

**Yes, every entity legally required to join pagoPA remains obliged to do so even if it does not receive payments from citizens and businesses, but only from public entities**. Therefore, state-controlled companies or public service providers and any other obliged entity that is not required to make payment transactions to other public administrations via fund transfers, must join pagoPA for the management of its revenues. In fact, neither the new art. 5 of the CAD, nor paragraph 5-bis of art. 15 of Decree-Law. 179/2012 specify that pagoPA exclusively concerns relationships with citizens or businesses. Therefore, all payments to entities required to join pagoPA must be made through pagoPA.

## How to reconcile debt positions related to the Tari/Tefa payment, for which the Province/Metropolitan City is the beneficiary?

The [D. M. 21 October 2020](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2020/11/06/20A05962/sg) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance has defined the methods for the unified payment, for the years 2021 and following, of the waste tax (TARI), the corresponding fee, and the tax for the exercise of environmental protection, preservation, and sanitation functions (TEFA) through the pagoPA platform. In consideration of what is established by this provision, PagoPA SpA has introduced a new method for generating the so-called 'multi-beneficiary' notice which, for a single payment, involves the pro-rata crediting of the amounts to the respective taxing authorities - Municipalities (which will have an active role) for TARI and Province/Metropolitan City/Region (which will have a passive role) for TEFA. Therefore, the Province/Metropolitan City, as the taxing authority for TEFA, must communicate the IBAN for crediting the TEFA tax to the municipalities in its territory, taking care to verify that this IBAN corresponds to one of those registered on the pagoPA Platform (cf. [https://developer.pagopa.it/pago-pa/guides/manuale-bo-ec](https://developer.pagopa.it/pago-pa/guides/manuale-bo-ec)).

Following the payment made by the user, the Province/Metropolitan City will be able to report TEFA collections analytically only if it is active on the broadcast station of the new model 3 'multi-beneficiary' by performing a cumulative reconciliation by Tax Code of the primary beneficiary Entity (Municipality) without the need to reconcile each individual debt position point by point. In other words, the adaptation of the secondary beneficiary Entity (Province/Metropolitan City) to the new model 3 - 'Multi-Beneficiary' is a necessary precondition for the cumulative reconciliation by Tax Code of the primary beneficiary Entity (Municipality) without the need for point-by-point reconciliation of each individual debt position.

PagoPA S.p.A., to facilitate reconciliation by the Provinces, has included specific metadata in the reconciliation flow. The minimum set of information provided consists of:

- BUDGET CHAPTER: element of the CE's budget to which the revenue is allocated;
- BUDGET ITEM: element of the CE's budget to which the revenue is allocated;
- ASSESSMENT CODE: code to identify the assessment;
- REFERENCE YEAR: reference year.

The Entity, therefore, will have to proceed in a different way than for its other collections and, to be able to use this simplified solution, it must necessarily integrate with the new single model.

Conversely, pending adaptation to the new model, the Province/Metropolitan City will only be able to carry out reconciliation operations manually and after requesting the list of receipts from the individual beneficiary Entity that issued the positions. In fact, if the Province/Metropolitan City has not yet activated the broadcast station on the new model 3 Multi-Beneficiary, it is necessary for the latter to request the list of receipts from each individual beneficiary Entity that issued the positions, performing the reconciliation manually. In other words, it will be necessary to verify the correspondence between the total TEFA amount declared by the main beneficiary Entity and what is received in the reporting flows by the secondary beneficiary Entity.
