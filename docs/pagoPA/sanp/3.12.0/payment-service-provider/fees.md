# Commissions

The services offered by the various PSPs participating in the pagoPA platform, with particular reference to payment commission costs, must be offered to the end-user through the touchpoints of PagoPA S.p.A., ensuring that all participating PSPs have the same opportunities for competition, equal treatment, and non-discrimination. This means that the display order of the PSPs must be random and the logo of the service provided must be shown, together with the logo of the PSP displaying it, next to the text that identifies the service itself.

Information regarding the commissions applied by PSPs is sent to the pagoPA platform through the "Catalogo Dati Informativi" support data structure within the "valoreCommissione" element.

To allow all PSPs to comply with banking transparency regulations, for all payers who do not have an existing framework agreement with the specific PSP selected on the platform, it is necessary that:

- each individual PSP populates an information sheet template for the payment services made available on the pagoPA platform; this template is prepared by PagoPA S.p.A.;
- on the pagoPA platform product website, an active link pointing to the respective information sheet must be displayed for each PSP, so that the information about the payment transaction on the pagoPA platform is available in pre- and/or post-contractual mode for the individual remote transaction;
- in order to verify constant compliance between what is indicated in the individual information sheet and what is displayed by PagoPA S.p.A. on the pagoPA platform, specifically on the Checkout component, and to prevent PagoPA S.p.A. from displaying information different from that indicated by the PSP in its own information sheet, it is specified that for information regarding commission costs present in the Catalogo Dati Informativi, the information sheet will refer to a different section of the pagoPA platform product website, where this information will be displayed to potential payers through a specific functionality.
