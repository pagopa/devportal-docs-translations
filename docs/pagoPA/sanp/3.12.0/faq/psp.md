# PSP

## What is meant by cash payments made at the entity that provides the treasury or cash management service for that entity?

**Paragraph 5 of the** [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) **lists the payment services that can still be managed in derogation of the mandatory use of the pagoPA platform,** and among these, letter d) refers exclusively to cash payments made at the bank that provides the treasury and cash management service for the Creditor Entity. Therefore, payment transactions made at physical POS terminals installed at the Creditor Entity's premises are not included under letter d), as they are not cash payments, nor are cash payments made at PSPs other than the treasurer or cashier bank, as they are not payments made at the bank providing the treasury and cash management service for the Creditor Entity. In fact, the management of the Entity's cash is the exclusive responsibility of the treasurer or cashier bank, and it refers exclusively to payments made in cash at that bank.

## Are payments via MAV still allowed?

**No,** **paragraph 5 of the** [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) **lists the payment services that can still be managed outside the pagoPA system, and MAVs are not among them, for which, starting from February 28, 2021, the ban will be effective** (Art. 65, paragraph 2, L. lgs no. 217/2017) **for PSPs to process such payment notices outside the pagoPA system,** where the beneficiary is a subject required to join pagoPA.

## How does the fund transfer by Poste Italiane to the treasury account work?

Paragraph 9.1 of the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) 'Payments made via postal current account slip' establishes the obligation of Poste Italiane S.p.A., if requested by the Creditor Entity, to transfer the sums collected through the pagoPA platform during a single business day to the treasury account. This procedure only pertains to transactions carried out on pagoPA by Poste Italiane with postal payment slips and not to other transactions carried out by other PSPs. In fact, this procedure is functional so that the postal account, when used as a 'support current account,' maintains the entity's ability to perform reconciliation through the cash journal and the pagoPA reconciliation/single RT flows. Therefore, for valid management of pagoPA flows and to achieve automated reconciliation, it is recommended that Creditor Entities with a postal account ancillary to the treasury account always populate both the 'Crediting C/A' field with the treasury IBAN and the 'Support C/A' field with the postal IBAN in their RPTs. This way, PSPs adhering to pagoPA will credit only the treasury account, with the exception of Poste Italiane S.p.A., which will instead use only the postal account and, if requested by the Entity, will also automatically transfer the funds from this account to the treasury account at the frequency requested by the Creditor Entity; all of which clearly simplifies the process of reconciliation and regularization of the collections received.

## Is a Creditor Entity adhering to the pagoPA System required to have a postal current account?

Paragraph 5 of the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) specifies that 'In order to allow the end-user to have all payment instruments available, including the postal payment slip service, every Creditor Entity, if it has a postal current account relationship, must register its IBAN on the pagoPA System, together with the treasury or cash current account'; hence the Entity's obligation to register at least one postal current account if it is already available to that Entity before joining pagoPA or, in any case, if the Entity has voluntarily decided to open one afterwards. In other words, if an Entity, for any reason, has a postal current account in its name, it must also use it for pagoPA. The same paragraph continues by specifying that 'For the same purpose, every Creditor Entity remains free to establish a postal current account relationship, even after joining the pagoPA System'; hence the option to establish a postal current account relationship, if such a relationship did not pre-exist joining pagoPA. Finally, the same paragraph of the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) points out that 'Every Creditor Entity, should it have other bank or postal current account relationships, may register their respective IBANs on the SPC-Payments Hub'; hence the further option for the Entity to also register more than one postal current account. Therefore, there is no obligation to establish a new relationship with Poste Italiane S.p.A. after joining pagoPA, but it is clear that such a relationship can expand the payment instruments that an entity can make available to payers through pagoPA.

The obligation to register the postal current account on the pagoPA System as per paragraph 5 of the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG) does not, however, imply a further obligation for the Creditor Entity to maintain said current account over time, thus leaving the Entity free to decide in this regard.

## Are existing treasury service agreements between PAs and PSPs still valid?

Conventions and/or agreements in place between a Public Administration and one or more Payment Service Providers, even if they concern electronic collection activities not consistent with the [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG), are valid until their natural expiration, provided they were stipulated before the legal deadline of 02/28/2021, without prejudice to the Entity's right to withdraw from the contract before its expiration to use pagoPA's features.

## Does the pagoPA system guarantee compliance with the ban on surcharging?

PSD and PSD2, i.e., **the European directives on payment services in the European Area** (SEPA), and likewise the respective national implementation legislation, **prohibit citizens from being charged a surcharge requested by the beneficiary for the execution of the payment, the so-called surcharge.**

The **pagoPA system is an ambitious, strategic, and innovative project that simplifies relationships, enhancing transparency, competition, and autonomy**, both in the public and private sectors, and aims to make the public sector more effective without sacrificing cost-containment policies.

With pagoPA, **the user is no longer required to make the payment according to the instructions given by the individual Creditor Entity, but can choose how to pay from numerous solutions offered freely, and competitively, by the PSP market.** Therefore, with the goal of overturning the previous logic of collection by the PA - which involved an agreement between the PA itself and a PSP as its special collector, with limitations for the user and costs for the PA as well as for the payer - with the pagoPA system, all participating PSPs can execute payments to Creditor Entities, leveraging their own contractual relationships (occasional or otherwise) with the paying user, without any need for any kind of agreement from the Creditor Entity. The PSP executing the payment, therefore, acts, whether occasionally or not, as the payer's provider and not as the provider of the beneficiary Creditor Entity.

The **pagoPA** **System, in fact, does not provide for any type of contractual relationship between the PSP and the Creditor Entity, so commissions are applied to the citizen by their PSP (freely selected from the participating PSPs) for the payment service they requested.** Consequently, in application of the so-called EU tariff principle. Share and the ban on surcharging, the payer is required to pay the commissions to the PSP they selected. Given how pagoPA works, these principles are also respected in card payment operations.

## Within the pagoPA system, which documents must be legally preserved?

Preservation is the activity aimed at protecting, over time, electronic documents and the data they contain, ensuring, among other things, their security, integrity, and non-modifiability, in order to preserve the evidentiary value of the electronic document and, in the specific case of pagoPA, of the payment transaction. In this context, examining the nature and characteristics of each of the elements involved in the pagoPA system flows, the focus is on:

- **the Telematic Payment Request**, which is generally the electronic document exchanged on the platform to issue the instruction from the Payer to the Payment Service Provider, containing all the elements required by the beneficiary Creditor Entity to trigger the payment process;
- **the Telematic Receipt**, which is generally the electronic confirmation of payment exchanged on the platform and prepared by the Payment Service Provider, guaranteeing the irrevocability of the payment, regardless of the instrument used;
- **the Reporting Flow**, which is the electronic document, prepared by the PSP and sent to the Creditor Entity, summarizing the payments received. This document, containing only the Unique Payment Identifier (IUV), has no external legal relevance but is used to facilitate the reconciliation of cumulatively received payments.

Considering that the payment receipt, provided by the Creditor Entity to the citizen, is created based on the receipt acquired from the pagoPA platform after the verification of the requested payment data by the CE itself, it is deemed appropriate, in order to keep a record of the entire payment transaction, to legally preserve all information flows of both the Telematic Receipt and the Telematic Payment Request, although this choice remains at the discretion of the individual CE.

## To join pagoPA, does my treasurer or cashier have to be a member as well?

\*\*Joining pagoPA is not connected to the treasury and cash management service. **When joining, the Entity must configure the IBANs of the bank and/or postal current accounts it intends to credit through pagoPA's features.** These current accounts can be credited regardless of whether their Payment Service Provider is a member of pagoPA. If the Entity's treasurer or cashier is a pagoPA member, they can support the Entity in joining, potentially acting as the Entity's Technological Intermediary (see. [Guidelines](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/04/16/24A01868/SG)). It should be noted that each Entity is free to determine how it procures goods and services, including technological intermediation services for joining pagoPA, always in compliance with the relevant regulations, including the Public Procurement Code and purchases on the platform managed by CONSIP S.p.A.

## What is meant by volume aggregation?

**Volume aggregation** - with other PSPs from the same corporate group or with other PSPs having the same Qualified Mandatary - **allows individual PSPs to have a lower average price per transaction, based on the higher tier reached during the reference period.** The total transactions aggregated in the billing quarter will then be distributed as follows:

- by PagoPA S.p.A. among the individual PSPs belonging to the same corporate group, in proportion to the number of transactions executed by the individual PSP;
- by the Qualified Mandatary, according to the distribution criteria agreed upon between the latter and the PSP when the mandate was conferred.

## How can a PSP use an Intermediary?

As provided for in Art. 6, paragraph 2 of the membership agreement, **an individual PSP can designate a Technological Intermediary for each payment service it provides.** After the PSP is registered in the Reserved Area, it can delegate its Technological Intermediary, just as CEs do.

## How does membership without a Mandatary work with channels for which the PSP has an Intermediary?

The role of **Qualified Mandatary concerns the contractual component** (in particular, the calculation of the fee due to PagoPA S.p.A. and who will pay this fee to PagoPA S.p.A.), **while the role of Technological Intermediary concerns the technical/infrastructural component for operating on the pagoPA platform.** The two components are autonomous and separate from each other, although a subject must be already operational as a Technological Intermediary to assume the role of Mandatary.

## Can the choice of whether or not to have a Mandatary be changed over time?

**A member PSP can freely change its choices over time, with the obligation to notify PagoPA S.p.A.,** which will implement them according to the timelines specified in the Service Agreement for joining the pagoPA electronic payments system.

## Are PSPs accredited on the Hub considered Accounting Agents under Art. 74 of Royal Decree. 2440/1923?

The payment scheme via the pagoPA platform involves the participation of:

- the **Public Administration, Publicly Controlled Company, or Public Service Operator**, as the beneficiary of the payment made by the citizen/business;
- the **debtor's PSP**, as the entity that debits the latter's account and orders the credit to the account of the Public Administration's or Public Service Operator's PSP, which is already contracted through the treasury agreement (or the current account opening contract in the case of BancoPosta).

In this operational model, since there is no collection activity by third parties and transfer from the third party to the beneficiary, **no entity qualifies as an Accounting Agent.**

## With pagoPA membership, do the execution times for payment transactions change for PSPs?

The Guidelines, as secondary legislation, are based on the primary provisions regarding payments, including Legislative Decree. no. 11/2010. Furthermore, **the agreement by which a Payment Service Provider joins pagoPA does not in any way address the execution times of payment transactions, which will, therefore, be governed by the primary reference legislation on payments, including Legislative Decree.** **no. 11/2010, without any possibility of derogation.** Moreover, it should be noted that the payer is always considered a customer, albeit occasional, of the PSP executing the payment transaction, and therefore, this transaction is governed—regarding its timing—by Article 20, paragraph 1, of Legislative Decree. no. 11/2010, which requires that the payment transaction be executed on the next business day (T+1) following the payer's request. Therefore, pagoPA also standardizes and unifies at a national level the timelines within which Entities receive funds paid for any reason by citizens and businesses into their current accounts, uniformly reducing this timeline to T+1.
