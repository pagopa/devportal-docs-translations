# App IO

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/appIO_SANP (4).png>)

- the user who starts the payment from the IO app <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt="" data-size="line"> must scan the QR code on the payment notice;
- the [paVerifyPaymentNotice](../../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#paverifypaymentnotice) is used to request the EC to verify the payment option identified by the notice number;
- the payment activation request reaches the EC via the [paGetPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pagetpayment);
- once the user has completed the actual payment operations, the Node sends the details to the PSP via the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment); in case of a KO response from the PSP, the process is interrupted and the payment must be reversed;
- in the event of technical exceptions during the call to pspNotifyPayment that prevent a response from being received, the Node will wait for the first of the following events to occur:
  - expiration of the **payment token:** the process is interrupted and the payment must be reversed;
  - receipt of the **sendPaymentOutcome vers. 2**: the flow proceeds normally, always keeping in mind that the PSP cannot send an outcome = KO;
- if the PSP sends a [sendPaymentOutcome](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome) after responding with a KO to the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment), the Node will respond with a KO to signal the conflicting outcome;
- in case of acceptance of the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment), the PSP is required to provide the payment outcome **within 2sec** with the [sendPaymentOutcome](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#sendpaymentoutcome); if the PSP sends an outcome = KO, the Node will respond with a KO to signal the conflicting outcome;
- via the [paSendRT](../../appendix/Primitives/Creditor-Entity/api-soap.md#pasendrt) primitive, the _receipt_ (ricevuta) is forwarded to the _n_ ECs involved in the payment only if the payment has been made; the _receipt_ is an object generated by the pagoPA platform;
- when the EC receives the _receipt_, it must close the debt position and consider it fully settled.

For error handling, refer to [Error Management](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/mU2qgiLV1G3m9z1VjAOc/ "mention").

For the correct and standardized use of _metadata_, a dedicated [Metadata Dictionary](https://app.gitbook.com/o/KXYtsf32WSKm6ga638R3/s/u6YdY319vyFX9MIvnKBa/ "mention") has been created, which includes a section dedicated to payment channel information present in the _additionalPaymentInformations_ of the [pspNotifyPayment](../../appendix/Primitives/psp/soap-api.md#pspnotifypayment).
