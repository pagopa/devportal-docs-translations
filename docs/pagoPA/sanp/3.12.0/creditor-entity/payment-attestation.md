# Payment Receipt

Every payment transaction is attested by the generation (and delivery) to the CE of a _receipt_ generated by the platform following the validation activity performed by PagoPA on the information acquired from the involved parties (CE and PSP).

The CE must make the _receipt_ available, upon the end-user's request, both as a digital duplicate and as an analog copy of it.

In addition to the pagoPA system logo, the analog copies produced must contain at least the following information:

- _date and time of the transaction:_ this means the date and time when the end-user started the payment transaction on the platform and is useful for the user's proof of payment;
- _Application date:_ this is the date on which the payment was registered within the PSP selected for the payment and determines the business day (cf. Guidelines and its definition in the SACI) on which the payment transaction falls;
- tax code of the EC (_fiscalCodePA_)
- name of the EC (_companyName_)
- unique payment identifier, the unique identifier assigned by the CE (_creditorReferenceId_)
- identifier of the PSP (at least company name and tax code)
- unique payment number (_paymentToken_)
- transaction amount (_totalAmount_)
- reason for payment (_paymentDescription_)
