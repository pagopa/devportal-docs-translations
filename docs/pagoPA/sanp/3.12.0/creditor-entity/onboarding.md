# Onboarding

To join pagoPA as a Creditor Entity (EC), Public Administrations, public service providers, and state-controlled companies must use the Reserved Area made available by PagoPA S.p.A. (more info here [https://docs.pagopa.it/area-riservata/area-riservata/come-aderire](https://docs.pagopa.it/area-riservata/area-riservata/come-aderire)).

Onboarding a Creditor Entity's collection service to pagoPA is not limited to the mere technical integration between the Entity's Applications and the pagoPA payments hub. The Entity is also required to ensure that:

- the collection for services is correctly reconciled in the Budget (through appropriate integrations with Management Software) as required by tax regulations, specifically concerning the taxonomic code of the collected service.
- citizens are correctly informed of the new payment opportunity at all contact points in order to maximize awareness of the new method.
- the Entity, directly or through a technology partner/intermediary, must ensure compliance with the SANP guidelines adopted by PagoPA S.p.A., and their constant updating

Creditor Entities (ECs), during the activation process on the pagoPA system, must use the Reserved Area Portal and the 'BackOffice pagoPA' vertical product, which are the tools made available to all parties involved in the process with different roles:

- the Payments Contacts appointed by the ECs;
- the Technical Contacts (and their delegates) designated by ECs directly connected to the pagoPA platform and by Technology Partners;
- the operators of the payments platform;
- PagoPA S.p.A.

The Payments Contact (RP) is the person appointed by the EC, through delegation from the legal representative, to operate within the pagoPA system to:

- specify and manage the EC's connections to the platform;
- appoint the Technical Contact, in case of a direct connection of the Entity to the platform;
- manage the IBANs of the credit accounts that the EC intends to use for collecting the amounts due;
- access the trouble-ticketing system made available by PagoPA S.p.A.;
- provide PagoPA S.p.A. with all the necessary data to enable other subjects belonging to the same Entity to use the trouble-ticketing system made available by PagoPA S.p.A.

The same Payments Contact can be appointed by multiple ECs.

The Technical Contact (RT) is the technical point of reference for an entity directly connected to the pagoPA platform (EC or Technology Partner). Each EC connection requires a Technical Contact: either the one appointed by the EC's Payments Contact (in case of a direct connection) or the one designated by the Technological Intermediary/Partner (in case of an intermediated connection).

The Technical Contact will be the same for all entities that use the Intermediary or Technology Partner that appointed them.

The Technical Contact will be enabled to access the trouble-ticketing system made available by PagoPA S.p.A. in order to improve and optimize communication between the ECs and Technology Partners connected to the pagoPA system and its dedicated Assistance Service.

It will also be the responsibility of the Technical Contact to provide all the necessary data to enable other members of their technical team to access the trouble-ticketing system made available by PagoPA S.p.A.

## Onboarding via a Technological Intermediary <a href="#id-174vkd27dh7y" id="id-174vkd27dh7y"></a>

The Payments Contact of an EC that has decided to connect to the platform by delegating technical activities to a Technological Intermediary must:

- register a connection with the chosen Technological Intermediary on the BackOffice pagoPA;
- activate all credit IBANs

For all other activities, the EC will refer to the person designated by the chosen Intermediary, without the authority to appoint or replace them, due to the delegation of technical activities.

To activate an EC, or rather its connection, the Technical Contact of the Technological Intermediary will use a specific feature of the BackOffice pagoPA that will allow them to provide PagoPA S.p.A. with all the required data. For all details regarding the activities to be performed on the BackOffice pagoPA, please refer to the specific user manual.

## Onboarding using a Technology Partner <a href="#godgz71r40mh" id="godgz71r40mh"></a>

The Payments Contact of an EC that has decided to connect to the platform by delegating technical activities to a Technology Partner must:

- register a connection intermediated by the chosen Technology Partner on the BackOffice pagoPA;
- activate all the Entity's credit IBANs

For all other activities, the EC will refer to the person designated by the chosen Technology Partner, without the authority to appoint or replace them, due to the delegation of technical activities.

The go-live process for an EC that has delegated to a Technology Partner is affected by the role the Entity plays in the activation of the Partner on the pagoPA system.

For a Technology Partner, an EC can be either the pilot Entity—the first EC for which the Partner activates one or more payment models—or a new EC joining those already intermediated by the Partner.

With reference to the above, please note that PagoPA S.p.A. could also be classified as a Technology Partner (see [Role and responsibilities of PagoPA S.p.A.](../implementation-Specifications-of-the-SPC-Payments-Hub/general-operation/roles.md#_rbtjsx52ef6i)).
