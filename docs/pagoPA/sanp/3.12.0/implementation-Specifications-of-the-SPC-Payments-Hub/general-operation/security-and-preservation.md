# Security and Preservation

All information processed within the system will be managed by the various actors interacting with the pagoPA platform, each within their own area of competence and responsibility, in compliance with current legislation on the preservation of digital documents and data security.

In this regard, it should be noted that the purpose of preservation is to protect digital documents and the data contained therein over time, ensuring, among other things, their integrity in order to preserve the evidentiary value of the digital document.

Without prejudice to the legal obligations concerning the keeping and preservation of documentation related to the activities carried out for the provision of the service and the use of its functions, as well as the provisions of current privacy legislation, every entity directly connected to the pagoPA platform (EC or PSP) is required to archive, without any modification, the data transmitted and received through the service.
