# General operation

The strategic objective of the pagoPA platform is to facilitate and promote, for the benefit of citizens and businesses, the use of advanced payment tools for the Public Administration, publicly controlled companies, and Public Service Operators.

The entities that have joined the pagoPA platform in implementation of art. 5 of the CAD are collectively called Creditor Body (EC).

The adherence to the pagoPA platform by all major PSPs, i.e., Payment Service Providers (Banks, Electronic Money Institutions, and Payment Institutions), allows their customers to access a vast and ever-expanding range of payment instruments. For example, holders of Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit cards, holders of a PayPal account and mobile payment apps, as well as users of Home banking services integrated with the pagoPA platform, either directly or through the MyBank service, can pay using the pagoPA platform.

Adherence to the pagoPA platform allows the Creditor Body to benefit from payment services without the need to establish an explicit relationship with the PSPs that provide them to their customers.

The enabling infrastructure that allows technical dialogue between ECs and PSPs is the pagoPA platform. Through this platform, the EC provides the PSP with the data necessary to provide the payment service and obtains, in a standardized manner and independent of the payment instrument used, the reporting data necessary for accounting reconciliation, thus simplifying back-office management processes.
