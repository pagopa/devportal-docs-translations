# Service Delivery and Service Levels

## Requirements During Service Delivery <a href="#aymo8ysmmt0h" id="aymo8ysmmt0h"></a>

All entities directly connected to the pagoPA Node must comply with the obligations and requirements described below.

### **Operational Desks**

All entities directly connected to the pagoPA platform are required to have an Operational Desk capable of providing the necessary support to detect and manage any operating anomalies.

The Technical Referent of the EC and the Technology Partner and the Referent of the PSP are required to provide PagoPA S.p.A. with all information relating to the Operational Desk, which constitutes a further point of contact in case of reports of anomalous operations.

**Operational Desks must be active 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.**

Technical Referents and PSP Referents are required to ensure that the Operational Desk is able to manage both ordinary operations (detection and management of specific operating anomalies) and emergency procedures to be activated in case of serious malfunctions.

PagoPA S.p.A. will provide the Operational Desks with a dedicated tool that will allow direct communication with the Help Desk.

### **Monitoring and Control**

Entities directly connected to the pagoPA platform must:

- use a system that monitors the service, which is also available to the Operational Desk;
- record and classify the reports received by the Operational Desk in order to facilitate the exchange of information with PagoPA S.p.A..

### **Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery**

Each entity directly connected to the pagoPA platform is required to prepare and implement technical and organisational solutions to ensure Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery, as required by current legislation (in particular in the “Digital Administration Code”, Legislative Decree no. 82/2005 and subsequent amendments. - arts. 50-bis and 51).

Should events occur that compromise Business Continuity, the entity is also required to promptly notify PagoPA S.p.A.

### **Further Requirements**

All ECs connected directly or indirectly to the pagoPA platform must:

- communicate to their end user any constraints and the availability of their services, with particular reference to payments activated at PSP facilities;
- communicate to the end user the typical characteristics of the payment services offered through the pagoPA platform;
- activate all payment services intended for the end user through the pagoPA system;
- respect the indicated service availability;
- maintain the availability of the Payments Referent and the Technical Referent figures and update PagoPA S.p.A. in the event of their replacement.

All PSPs connected directly or indirectly to the pagoPA platform must also:

- keep the Informational Data Catalogue updated;
- guarantee the availability of the Referent indicated in the Adhesion Agreement and inform PagoPA S.p.A. in case of his/her replacement;
- if they offer services at their own facilities and/or local service points, they must communicate this possibility to end users by displaying the specific “Logo” registered by PagoPA S.p.A. on site.

## Service Availability <a href="#xqrq1xhty79g" id="xqrq1xhty79g"></a>

Each entity adhering to the pagoPA system is required to comply with the service levels indicated in the appendix [quality-indicators-for-adhering-parties](../appendici/indicatori-di-qualita-per-i-soggetti-aderenti/ "mention").

PagoPA S.p.A. will periodically verify compliance with the service levels by the directly connected entity, reserving the right to take disciplinary action if non-compliance persists over time.
